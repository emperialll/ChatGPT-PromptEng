# Version 1 - Basic Prompt

Bring mid-century style and everyday comfort to your workspace with this versatile office chair. Designed to coordinate beautifully with a full family of office furniture—including desks, filing cabinets, bookcases, and meeting tables—it’s an elegant choice for both home offices and professional environments.

The chair features a smooth 5-wheel plastic-coated aluminum base and pneumatic height adjustment for easy raise/lower control throughout the day. Choose from a range of shell colors, base finishes, upholstery options, caster types, foam densities, and arm configurations to create the right fit for your space and working style.

Available with either a plastic back and upholstered front or full upholstery, this chair can be customized in multiple fabric and leather options. Base finishes include stainless steel, matte black, gloss white, and chrome. Select an armless design for a clean, minimal look or add 8-position PU armrests for adjustable support.

## Details

- Mid-century inspired office chair suitable for home or business use
- Qualified for contract use
- 5-wheel plastic-coated aluminum base
- Pneumatic seat height adjustment
- Available with or without armrests
- Soft or hard-floor caster options
- Medium or high-density seat foam options
- HD36 foam seat
- Cast aluminum shell base glider with modified nylon coating
- Made in Italy
