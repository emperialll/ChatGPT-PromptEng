This mid-century inspired office chair is engineered for both home and contract environments, offering a durable material specification with flexible finish and upholstery options. The chair features a 5-wheel plastic-coated aluminum base with pneumatic height adjustment for smooth raise/lower functionality. The shell base glider is constructed from cast aluminum with a modified nylon PA6/PA66 coating, with a shell thickness of 10 mm for structural stability.

Seating comfort is supported by HD36 foam, with available medium-density foam at 1.8 lb/ft³ or high-density foam at 2.8 lb/ft³. Upholstery configurations include a plastic back with front upholstery or full upholstery, available in multiple fabric and leather options. Base finishes include stainless steel, matte black, gloss white, and chrome. The chair is also available armless or with 8-position PU armrests, and can be specified with soft- or hard-floor casters.
